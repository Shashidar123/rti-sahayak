🧭 RTI Application — Timeline & Stages

Use this general workflow after identifying the correct Public Authority.

1
Identify the information

Clearly describe the records or information you want.

2
Identify the Public Authority

Choose the correct government department or public authority.

3
Find the PIO / APIO

Use the authority's official RTI information to identify the appropriate officer.

4
Prepare the application

Ask for existing records, documents, data, reports, orders or other information held by the authority.

5
Pay the applicable fee

Check the State/UT and authority-specific fee and permitted payment mode.

6
Submit & preserve proof

Keep the acknowledgement, registration number, payment receipt and a copy of the application.

7
Response / next step

If the response is missing, incomplete or unsatisfactory, consider the applicable first appeal and further remedies.

Central vs State: The Central RTI Online portal is intended for Central Government public authorities. Do not use it for a State Government public authority; use the applicable State/UT filing route instead.

📄 Documents, Fees & BPL Guidance

Requirements vary by jurisdiction and public authority. Check the selected State/UT guide before filing.

🪪

Applicant details

Keep your name and contact/address details ready. Fill in any required fields before submission.

💳

Application fee

Pay the applicable initial RTI application fee using the payment method permitted by the concerned jurisdiction/authority.

📑

Supporting documents

Attach documents only when they are relevant or specifically required. An RTI request is primarily a request for information/records.

♿

BPL applicants

Fee exemption may apply to eligible BPL applicants subject to the applicable rules. Keep the relevant BPL proof available where required.

🧾

Keep filing proof

Save the application copy, payment proof, acknowledgement/registration number and postal or online submission details.

⚠️

Fees can have more than one component

The initial application fee is not necessarily the only possible charge. Copying, inspection or other prescribed charges may apply.

Important: The fee shown on each State/UT card is the configured application fee in RTI Sahayak. Before payment, verify the current fee and payment method on the official authority/portal.

⚖️ Appeals, Lawyer Clarification & Official Resources

1️⃣

First Appeal

If the RTI response is not received or is not satisfactory, an applicant may use the applicable first-appeal mechanism before the designated First Appellate Authority.

2️⃣

Second Appeal / Complaint

Further statutory remedies may be available before the appropriate Information Commission, depending on the circumstances.

👤

Do I need a lawyer?

You generally do not need a lawyer to file an RTI application or use the normal RTI appeal process. Citizens can represent themselves.
